About NOHS

Negros Occidental High School (NOHS) is a public school in Bacolod City serving around 9,000 learners in Grades 7 to 12.

Junior and Senior High School share one campus, so students can grow with the same community from their first year to graduation. Learners follow a strong core curriculum in Grades 7 to 10, then choose from the STEM, ABM, HUMSS, GAS and TVL strands in Senior High School.

Our teachers and staff work together so that every student feels known, challenged and supported. We are led by our principal, Dr. Cheryl Juson Tondo, and we invite families to visit, ask questions and see how our community learns and serves together.

Our history

NOHS began in 1902 as Instituto Rizal, the first secondary school in the province, and has carried the name of Negros Occidental High School for most of its long story. It is often still called the "Provincial High School."

  1. 1902

    Instituto Rizal opens

    Classes begin in rented buildings, with Thomasite Ray Howell as first principal.

  2. 1927

    A permanent home

    Construction of the high school building at the present site is funded.

  3. 1931

    Move to the new campus

    Classes transfer to the new building, whose facade reads "Bacolod High School."

  4. 1970

    General comprehensive

    The school becomes Negros Occidental General Comprehensive High School.

  5. 1983

    Original name restored

    The school returns to the name Negros Occidental High School.

Our vision and mission

The ideals that guide our school.

Our Vision

We dream of Filipinos who passionately love their country and whose values and competencies enable them to realize their full potential and contribute meaningfully to building the nation.

As a learner-centered public institution, the Department of Education continuously improves itself to better serve its stakeholders.

Our Mission

To protect and promote the right of every Filipino to quality, equitable, culture-based, and complete basic education where:

  • Students learn in a child-friendly, gender-sensitive, safe, and motivating environment.
  • Teachers facilitate learning and constantly nurture every learner.
  • Administrators and staff, as stewards of the institution, ensure an enabling and supportive environment for effective learning to happen.
  • Family, community, and other stakeholders are actively engaged and share responsibility for developing life-long learners.

Our core values

The four values every NOHS learner is encouraged to live by.

  • Maka-Diyos

    Faith, honesty and respect for what is good and sacred.

  • Maka-tao

    Care, kindness and respect for every person.

  • Makakalikasan

    Responsibility for protecting our environment.

  • Makabansa

    Love of country and service to the nation.
